Babyspoon Privacy Policy

Effective: October 4, 2026 · Developer: SDDT (sanddeuts) · Contact: harrisleesh@gmail.com

English

Babyspoon ("the app") helps parents and caregivers log a baby's first foods, allergens, reactions, meals and freezer stock. We designed it to collect as little as possible.

1. Data you enter. Child profile (nickname, birthdate, weeks born early, weaning method), meals (foods, amounts, textures, reactions, notes, optional photos), reaction logs (symptoms, severity), food preferences and freezer cube inventory. Food reactions and symptoms are health-related information about a child; please use a nickname instead of a full name.

2. Where it is stored.

  • Solo mode (default): everything stays on your device, in the app's private storage. We (the developer) cannot see it. It is included in your device's own encrypted backups (iCloud / Google) according to your device settings.
  • Family sync (optional, only when you turn it on and only in builds where the service is available): the data above, your display name and an anonymous account ID are stored on our server (Supabase, region Seoul, Republic of Korea; encrypted in transit and at rest) so the family members you invite can see and edit them. Before turning it on, the app asks for your explicit consent. Members of a family can see that family's data; nobody else can (access is enforced by row-level security).

3. Purchases. In-app purchases are processed by Apple / Google. We use RevenueCat to check whether Premium is active; it receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase history. We do not receive your payment details.

4. What we do not do. No advertising, no ad identifiers, no analytics or tracking SDKs, no selling or sharing of data with data brokers. We never use your child's data for marketing or to train AI models.

5. Notifications. Reminders (observation windows, freezer cubes) are scheduled locally on your device.

6. Retention and deletion. Settings → "Delete all data" erases everything on the device; when family sync is on it also deletes your account, your memberships and families where you are the last member, including photos, from the server. Uninstalling the app removes solo-mode data. You can also email us to request deletion.

7. Children. The app is intended for adults (parents and caregivers), not for children to use. We do not knowingly collect data from children who use the app themselves.

8. Your rights. Depending on where you live (e.g. GDPR, Korea PIPA, Japan APPI, US state laws) you may request access, correction, deletion or a copy of your data. Contact harrisleesh@gmail.com. Data stored for family sync is processed in the Republic of Korea.

9. Medical information. The app provides general information only and is not a medical device.

10. Changes. We will post updates on this page and change the effective date.
